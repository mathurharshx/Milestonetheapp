# Privacy Policy for Milestone

**Last Updated:** September 2026

At **Milestone** ("we", "our", or "the app"), we believe your focus, goals, and daily tasks belong to you and nobody else. Milestone is built from the ground up with a privacy-first architecture:

### 1. Zero Data Collection
- We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.
- We do **not** use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
- We do **not** track your location, browsing habits, or app activity.

### 2. 100% On-Device Storage
- All your missions, tasks, pomodoro sessions, custom soundscape settings, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device.
- Widget data is shared only between the Milestone main app and the Milestone Widget Extension via a local iOS App Group container on your device.
- If you delete the app, all local data is removed.

### 3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
- Subscriptions and purchases (Milestone Premium) are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2 and your Apple ID.
- We do not receive, process, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address. All transaction validation occurs directly through Apple's secure infrastructure.

### 4. Audio & Permissions
- **Soundscapes**: Procedural soundscapes (40Hz Gamma waves and Brown Noise) are generated mathematically in real-time on your device using Apple's AVAudioEngine. No audio is recorded, accessed from external servers, or transmitted.
- **Notifications**: Local notifications for timers and morning reminders are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device without communicating with any external push notification server.
